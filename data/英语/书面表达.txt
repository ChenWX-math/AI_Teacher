# 英语书面表达

## 一、写作四步法

Step 1 — 审题：确定文体（书信/议论文/记叙文）、人称、时态、要点
Step 2 — 列提纲：每个要点一句话，搭好框架
Step 3 — 写作：提纲扩充成文，加连接词和高级表达
Step 4 — 检查：拼写、语法、时态、字数（100词左右）

## 二、常见文体模板

### 1. 书信/邮件
开头：I'm writing to...（写信目的）
正文要点：First of all... / Besides... / What's more...
结尾：I'm looking forward to your reply.

### 2. 议论文
第一段：观点陈述（In my opinion,... / I believe that...）
第二段：理由1 + 论据 / 理由2 + 论据（For one thing... For another...）
第三段：总结（In conclusion,... / To sum up,...）

### 3. 记叙文
第一段：时间、地点、人物（交代背景）
第二段：事件经过（按时间顺序）
第三段：感悟/收获（I learned that...）

### 4. 通知
开头：May I have your attention, please? / Notice
正文：时间、地点、内容、要求
结尾：落款和时间

## 三、连接词（提升流畅度）

### 表顺序
First, Second, Third / To begin with, Furthermore, Finally

### 表转折
However, Nevertheless, On the contrary, In spite of

### 表因果
Therefore, As a result, Consequently, Due to

### 表递进
Moreover, What's more, In addition, Besides

### 表举例
For example, For instance, Such as, Take...as an example

### 表总结
In conclusion, To sum up, In a word, All in all

## 四、高级表达替换

| 普通表达 | 高级表达 |
|---------|---------|
| I think | From my perspective / As far as I'm concerned |
| very important | vital / essential / significant / crucial |
| many | a variety of / a great number of |
| like | be fond of / be keen on / be crazy about |
| because | due to / owing to / on account of |
| should | be supposed to / be expected to |
| but | however / nevertheless / whereas |
| help | assist / do sb a favor / lend a hand |
| I know | I'm aware of / I'm well-informed about |
| good | outstanding / remarkable / fantastic |

## 五、高分技巧

### 1. 句式多样化
简单句：I like reading.
并列句：I like reading, and my brother likes sports.
复合句：I like reading books that broaden my horizons.
强调句：It is reading that brings me joy.
倒装句：Not only does reading enrich knowledge, but it also brings fun.

### 2. 非谓语加分
- 分词作状语：Faced with difficulties, we should keep optimistic.
- 不定式表目的：To solve the problem, we need teamwork.
- 动名词作主语：Learning a foreign language takes time.

### 3. 谚语引用
- Practice makes perfect.（熟能生巧）
- Where there is a will, there is a way.（有志者事竟成）
- Every coin has two sides.（事物都有两面性）

## 六、易错点
- 主谓不一致（第三人称单数忘加 s）
- 时态混用（记叙文统一过去时，议论文一般现在时）
- 句子不完整（只有从句没有主句）
- 中式英语（按照中文语序直译）
- 字数不够或超太多
